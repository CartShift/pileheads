Your hand
Exchange a hand card with a card on the row. Any 3+ consecutive same-colored cards are captured.

Physical-playtest assumptions: Cards are drawn from one finite, balanced deck; scoring removes matching runs and closes the gaps; refill rejects any top card that would make an automatic triple (with a visible discard pile). This is a provisional rule, and exhausted-deck behavior is still being evaluated.

In Exchange, cards of the same color cannot be swapped because that would be a no-op. In Insert, one hand card is placed in a gap and replaced from the stock. Games are eight turns per person. The highest captured-card count wins.
