# SPARK Exchange tabletop reference sheet (candidate, not final retail rules)

**Equipment prototype:** 144 ordinary cards, 36 each in orange ●, blue ◆, purple ✦, green ▲. Three cards per player (2–4 players). Nine face-up cards in one row. One draw pile and one discard pile. No special powers or bonus counters. Use small card size (Mini Euro ~44×68mm) for the nine-card row. All score piles are face up.

1. Shuffle all cards, deal each player three. Then build nine face-up cards in a single line. If drawing the next card would create three identical colors at the right edge, place it face-up in the discard pile and draw another.
2. On your turn, exchange **one card from your hand** with **one card of a different color in the row**, anywhere in the row. Keep the removed row card in your hand. The row stays nine cards before any scoring.
3. Whenever three or more **adjacent cards of the same color** appear, remove that run and collect those cards in your score pile. Close the gap. Repeat if the collapse creates another run. No extra points or mathematics.
4. Draw from the face-down pile and append cards to the **right** until the row has nine cards again. If an appended card would immediately make a triple, reject it face up into the discard pile and try the next. Reshuffle discards into a face-down stock if necessary. **Open release blocker:** define how to finish/refill if no safe card can be drawn, rather than getting stuck.
5. After everyone has played eight turns, count the cards in each player's scoring pile. **Most cards wins**; equal highest totals tie.

**Do not invent an extra move:** you exchange exactly one hand card, whether or not you score. An identical-color swap is illegal (it would leave the state unchanged).

**Playtest alternative:** Use seven row cards rather than nine, keeping the same rules and hand size. Compare both using the prototype.

**Question for players:** Which move did you seriously consider but *not* take? What would your opponent have gained from it?
