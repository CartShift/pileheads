Exchange Lab

How to play

1 Choose one of your 3 cards
2 Swap it with a different-colored row card
3 Capture 3+ matching cards to score

You do not add a card to the row. The card you choose replaces one row card. That row card comes into your hand. You always finish your turn holding 3 cards.

Example: if the row shows ◆◆● and you replace the orange card with your blue card, the three blue cards are captured for 3 points. You keep the orange card.

After 10 turns each, whoever captured the most cards wins. There are no bonus turns or attacks.

Try Open Hands: see your opponent's three cards, plan traps, deny their best swaps and choose which colors to bring back into your hand.

Game settings

The deck is finite and balanced: four colors, 36 cards each. There are no special cards or mandatory discard decisions. An exchange that leaves the same card in place is illegal.

Compare with Momentum. This is a separate experimental game, not an update to SPARK Simple+.
