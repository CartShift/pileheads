How do I play? Three simple rules
  1. Play one card with a number that follows the pile's arrow. ↑ means the same number or higher; ↓ means the same number or lower.
  2. Your card sets the next rule. The arrow printed on your card applies to the next player. Play the same number as the top card to clear the whole pile.
  3. Stuck? Take the pile's top card, clear the rest and end your turn. Keep three cards in hand while the draw deck lasts. Once it's empty, the first player to shed their entire hand wins.

This is a custom 48-card deck, not ordinary playing cards. There are six copies of each number (1–8), half ↑ and half ↓.
