Rules in 30 seconds
  1. Follow the arrow on the pile: ↑ means the same number or higher; ↓ means the same number or lower.
  2. Put down any playable card. Its arrow decides what the next person must play. Match the same number to clear the pile.
  3. If you are stuck, pick up the top card. Keep three cards in hand while the draw deck lasts. Once the deck is empty, the first to empty their hand wins.
