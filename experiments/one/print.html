TILT ONE · Print-and-play

48 original prototype cards, each with one number and one direction. Six A4 card sheets plus one-page rules. Use identical opaque-backed sleeves.

Back to the game
These are mechanical test cards, not final commercial artwork. Print at actual size, cut on dashed edges, and sleeve all cards with indistinguishable backs. Every number 1–8 has three ↑ and three ↓ cards. Two copies of each combination are marked CORE, and one EXTRA. For the short two-player game, use only the 32 CORE cards. Do not substitute a standard playing-card deck.
