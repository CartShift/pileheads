What should we evaluate?

Can you explain the rules after your first turn? Could you make the next player pick up cards on purpose? Did preparing a card give you a real rescue? Would you voluntarily play again? Tell us where the ending felt unfair or boring.

Physical-first design. Same original two-value deck for both concepts, not a 52-card deck reskin. No manufacturing or production deployment is approved by these previews.
