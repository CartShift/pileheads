CLAIM: your first printed prototype

72 cards: three colors × ranks 1–3 × eight copies. Keep colors and ranks legible; use identical opaque sleeves so shuffled hands are secret. Four-card hands, three shared color lanes, bank 3+ when leading. At five or more cards, banked points double.

The complete physical rules and playtest questions are in RULES.md and PLAYTEST.md (source files may need to be opened from GitHub if your static host only serves this print sheet).
