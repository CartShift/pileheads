The rules in four sentences:

Play one card into the nine-card row. Three identical colors in a row trigger an attack. You send one captured card to the other player (one per matching wave in Chains), and earn one optional free placement. If the bonus does not match, the rightmost row card is discarded instead of being taken into your hand. A bonus can attack but never earns another bonus. On a turn without a match, take the rightmost card into your hand instead of drawing.

Every card counts as one card. Empty your hand to win. There are no bonus points or special cards. Choose End bonus whenever you prefer not to play again.
