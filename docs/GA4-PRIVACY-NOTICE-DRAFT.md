# GA4 privacy disclosure — draft, not approved for publication

This section is intended for incorporation into the complete PILEHEADS privacy notice. It does not cover hosting, accounts, gameplay, shop or other processing. Do not publish or activate GA4 until the owner has confirmed the actual stream settings, controller contact details, Google processing terms, transfer safeguards and retention periods.

## Proposed visitor-facing text

### Optional website analytics

If you choose to allow website analytics, we use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors find PILEHEADS, which public pages they view and whether they follow links to play the game. You can use PILEHEADS without accepting analytics.

Google Analytics uses first-party cookies and device/browser information to measure interactions. Our implementation sends selected public page views, recognized campaign categories and clicks on links to play. We do not intentionally send player names, room codes, account identifiers or card contents to Google Analytics. Analytics is separate from optional first-party gameplay measurement.

The proposed legal basis is your consent under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR and, where applicable, Section 25(1) TDDDG for access to or storage of information on your device. You can change your choice through Analytics preferences at any time. Withdrawal applies to future collection and does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

Google Analytics is supplied by Google.
